# Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam — Canto 1, Chapter 2: Divinity and Divine Service

**Authorized source:** [Vedabase — SB 1.2](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/2/)

**Total:** 34 numbered verses; 34 one-word-named study folders.

## Chapter orientation

Sūta introduces devotional practice, its inward development, and Vāsudeva as life's ultimate aim.

Names below are editorial study themes, not authorized translations. Use Vedabase for the verses, translations, and purports. Existing study space for Bhakti Vaibhava assessments remains available through the personal notes in each verse folder.

**Naming:** `verse-02.01-gratitude` through `verse-02.34-protection`. Two digits follow the decimal.

**Combined source:** Vedabase presents texts 28–29 together; their separate study folders share that source link.
