**Total:** 38 numbered verses; 38 one-word-named study folders.

## Chapter orientation

Follow Nārada's recollections of childhood, solitary travel, devotional experience, and a life devoted to sacred song.

Names below are editorial study themes, not authorized translations. Consult Vedabase for the verses, translations, and purports. Use personal notes for reflection and Bhakti Vaibhava assessment preparation.

**Naming:** `verse-06.01-inquiry` through `verse-06.38-glorification` — two digits after the decimal.
