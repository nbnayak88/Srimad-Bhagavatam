# Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam — Canto 1, Chapter 5: Nārada’s Instructions on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam for Vyāsadeva

**Authorized source:** [Vedabase — SB 1.5](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/5/)

**Total:** 40 numbered verses; 40 one-word-named study folders.

## Chapter orientation

Explore Nārada's guidance to Vyāsa, his recollections of devotional beginnings, and the dedication of everyday action to divine service.

Names below are editorial study themes, not authorized translations. Consult Vedabase for the verses, translations, and purports. Use personal notes for reflection and Bhakti Vaibhava assessment preparation.

**Naming:** `verse-05.01-dialogue` through `verse-05.40-relief` — two digits after the decimal.
