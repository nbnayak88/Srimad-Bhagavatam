# Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam — Canto 1, Chapter 1: Questions by the Sages

**Authorized text:** [Vedabase — SB 1.1](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/1/)

**Total:** 23 numbered verses; 23 one-word-named study folders.

## Chapter orientation

The chapter opens with meditation on the Absolute Truth and praise of pure devotion and Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam. In the sacred Naimiṣāraṇya assembly, sages led by Śaunaka respectfully question Sūta Gosvāmī about humanity's ultimate welfare, the essence of scripture, Lord Kṛṣṇa's appearance and incarnations, the holy name, devotional hearing, and the shelter of religion after Kṛṣṇa's departure.

The one-word names below are **editorial study themes** informed by the chapter's individual numbered texts; they are not the authorized translations. Consult each linked Vedabase text and purport for definitive interpretation. The copyrighted translations and purports are not reproduced.

**Folder naming:** `verse-01.01-absoluteness` through `verse-01.23-shelter` — chapter and verse use two digits; the final word is the study theme.
