## Orientation

The sages’ questions, the purpose of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, and the opening of its teachings through Sūta Gosvāmī, Śukadeva Gosvāmī, and Parīkṣit Mahārāja.

Use the source page to navigate chapters, verses, translations, and purports. This README is a study guide and entry point, not a replacement for the text.

## Suggested learning rhythm

1. **Read:** Work through a manageable passage, using the verse and purport together.
2. **Observe:** Identify the speakers, context, central question, and main teaching.
3. **Question:** Note terms, philosophical points, or narrative details to revisit.
4. **Connect:** Look for relationships with earlier passages and the larger message of bhakti.
5. **Reflect:** Record one insight and one question for further study.
