# TEXT 7: Captains

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Captains

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 7](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/7/)

## Translation

But for your information, O best of the brāhmaṇas, let me tell you about the captains who are especially qualified to lead my military force.
