# TEXT 11: Protection

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Protection

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 11](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/11/)

## Translation

All of you must now give full support to Grandfather Bhīṣma, as you stand at your respective strategic points of entrance into the phalanx of the army.
