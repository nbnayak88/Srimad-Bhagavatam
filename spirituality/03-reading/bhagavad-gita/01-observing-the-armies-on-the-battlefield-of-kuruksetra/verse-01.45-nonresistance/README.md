# TEXT 45: Nonresistance

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Nonresistance

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 45](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/45/)

## Translation

Better for me if the sons of Dhṛtarāṣṭra, weapons in hand, were to kill me unarmed and unresisting on the battlefield.
