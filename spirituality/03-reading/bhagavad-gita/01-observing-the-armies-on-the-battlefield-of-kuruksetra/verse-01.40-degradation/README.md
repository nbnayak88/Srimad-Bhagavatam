# TEXT 40: Degradation

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Degradation

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 40](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/40/)

## Translation

When irreligion is prominent in the family, O Kṛṣṇa, the women of the family become polluted, and from the degradation of womanhood, O descendant of Vṛṣṇi, comes unwanted progeny.
