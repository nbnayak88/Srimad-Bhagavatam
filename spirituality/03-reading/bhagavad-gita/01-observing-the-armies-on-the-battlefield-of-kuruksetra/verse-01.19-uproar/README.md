# TEXT 19: Uproar

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Uproar

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 19](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/19/)

## Translation

The blowing of these different conchshells became uproarious. Vibrating both in the sky and on the earth, it shattered the hearts of the sons of Dhṛtarāṣṭra.
