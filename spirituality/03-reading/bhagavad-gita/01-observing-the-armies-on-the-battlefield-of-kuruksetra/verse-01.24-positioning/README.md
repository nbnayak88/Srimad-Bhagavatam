# TEXT 24: Positioning

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Positioning

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 24](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/24/)

## Translation

Sañjaya said: O descendant of Bharata, having thus been addressed by Arjuna, Lord Kṛṣṇa drew up the fine chariot in the midst of the armies of both parties.
