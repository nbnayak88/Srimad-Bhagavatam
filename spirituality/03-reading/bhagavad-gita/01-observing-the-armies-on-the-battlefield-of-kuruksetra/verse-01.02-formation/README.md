# TEXT 2: Formation

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Formation

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 2](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/2/)

## Translation

Sañjaya said: O King, after looking over the army arranged in military formation by the sons of Pāṇḍu, King Duryodhana went to his teacher and spoke the following words.
