# TEXT 43: Succession

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Succession

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 43](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/43/)

## Translation

O Kṛṣṇa, maintainer of the people, I have heard by disciplic succession that those whose family traditions are destroyed dwell always in hell.
