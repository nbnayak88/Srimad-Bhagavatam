# TEXT 30: Confusion

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Confusion

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 30](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/30/)

## Translation

I am now unable to stand here any longer. I am forgetting myself, and my mind is reeling. I see only causes of misfortune, O Kṛṣṇa, killer of the Keśī demon.
