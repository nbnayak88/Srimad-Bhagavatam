# TEXT 8: Veterans

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Veterans

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 8](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/8/)

## Translation

There are personalities like you, Bhīṣma, Karṇa, Kṛpa, Aśvatthāmā, Vikarṇa and the son of Somadatta called Bhūriśravā, who are always victorious in battle.
