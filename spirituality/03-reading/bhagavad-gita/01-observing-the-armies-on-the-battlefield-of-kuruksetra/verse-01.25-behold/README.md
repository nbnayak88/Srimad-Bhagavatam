# TEXT 25: Behold

**Chapter One: Observing the Armies on the Battlefield of Kurukṣetra**

**One-word theme:** Behold

**Vedabase:** [Bhagavad-gītā 25](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/1/25/)

## Translation

In the presence of Bhīṣma, Droṇa and all the other chieftains of the world, the Lord said, “Just behold, Pārtha, all the Kurus assembled here.”
