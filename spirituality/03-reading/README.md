## A reading session

1. Choose a passage and read its linked authorized source and purport.
2. Record one question, one connection, and one insight in your notes.
3. Explore related passages and revisit the question with a teacher or study group.
4. Try one practical application and reflect on what you learn.

Keep scripture, editorial themes, and personal interpretations distinct. Existing verse numbering and source references are preserved.
