# Chapter 8: Questions by King Parīkṣit

## Closed-Book Short Answer Questions

**Bhakti Vaibhava — Unit 8 | Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam Canto 2**

Source: Bhakti Vaibhava Students’ Handbook, Unit 8. [Vedabase Chapter 8](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/).

> Concise study paraphrases rather than verbatim purport quotations. Question order follows the handbook.

### 1. What is the significance of the word aguṇa? (2.8.1)

**Answer:** The Lord is beyond the three material modes; He nevertheless possesses eternal transcendental qualities.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.1](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/1/)

### 2. What is the test of a bona fide spiritual master in disciplic succession? (2.8.1)

**Answer:** His teaching agrees with the Lord's original message, transmitted without distortion through authorized paramparā.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.1](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/1/)

### 3. What does Mahārāja Parīkṣit recommend to help one see Lord Kṛṣṇa manifest within one's heart in no time? (2.8.4)

**Answer:** Faithful, attentive hearing of the Lord's glories from a bona fide devotee.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.4](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/4/)

### 4. Explain why Brahmā is called ajaḥ. (2.8.9)

**Answer:** He does not undergo ordinary birth from a mother's womb; he appears on the lotus originating from the Lord.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.9](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/9/)

### 5. Explain the analogies of American government restrictions and sitting on the chair of the high court judge. (2.8.14)

**Answer:** Government restrictions illustrate that subordinate freedom remains subject to higher law; a judge's chair represents delegated office, not independent supreme authority. Similarly, Brahmā's position depends on the Lord.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.14](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/14/)

### 6. List four of the eight mystic powers. (2.8.20)

**Answer:** Aṇimā (minuteness), laghimā (lightness), mahimā (greatness), and prāpti (obtaining desired things).

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.20](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/20/)
