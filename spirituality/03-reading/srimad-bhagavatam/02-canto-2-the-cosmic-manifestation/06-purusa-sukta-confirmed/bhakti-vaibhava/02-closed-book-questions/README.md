# Chapter 6: Puruṣa-sūkta Confirmed

## Closed-Book Questions

**Bhakti Vaibhava · Unit 8 · Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam Canto 2**

**Source:** Bhakti Vaibhava Students’ Handbook, Unit 8; [Vedabase Chapter 6](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/).

> Study-oriented paraphrases, not verbatim quotations. Questions follow the handbook order. The personal-reflection question includes an explicitly marked model answer. Check exact Sanskrit terms against the linked verse and purport.

### 1. Explain why this chapter is entitled 'Puruṣa-sūkta Confirmed'. (SB 2.6.23)

**Answer:** Brahmā describes the universal form and the cosmic sacrifice in terms consistent with the Vedic Puruṣa-sūkta. All beings, materials, offerings, and powers of sacrifice originate in the Supreme Puruṣa. Brahmā's sacrifice acknowledges the Lord as both the source and ultimate recipient of sacrifice.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.23](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/23/)

### 2. Explain the analogy of father and obedient son used in the purport to 2.6.6. (SB 2.6.6)

**Answer:** A dutiful son follows his father's guidance and uses what he receives responsibly. Similarly, the living being should recognize the Supreme Lord as the original source and use the Lord's gifts in His service, not claim independent proprietorship.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.6](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/6/)

### 3. Differentiate between liberated souls and conditioned souls as discussed in 2.6.17 purport. (SB 2.6.17)

**Answer:** Liberated souls act in their constitutional relationship of loving service to the Lord, free from the influence of the material modes. Conditioned souls identify with temporary bodies, seek material enjoyment, and remain subject to karma and rebirth.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.17](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/17/)

### 4. In the Caitanya-caritāmṛta, what are the total universes compared to? (SB 2.6.18)

**Answer:** The universes are compared to mustard seeds gathered in a bag, showing their minuteness in relation to the unlimited spiritual and material energies of the Lord.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.18](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/18/)

### 5. Describe some qualities of the planets in the spiritual sky and their inhabitants. (SB 2.6.18)

**Answer:** The Vaikuṇṭha planets are eternal, spiritual, free from material decay and the cycle of birth and death. Their inhabitants possess spiritual forms and engage in uninterrupted loving service to the Lord.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.18](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/18/)

### 6. State any three differences between pāda-vibhūti and tripāda-vibhūti. (SB 2.6.19)

**Answer:** (1) Pāda-vibhūti refers to the one-fourth material manifestation; tripāda-vibhūti refers to the three-fourths spiritual manifestation. (2) The material domain is temporary and subject to the modes; the spiritual domain is eternal and transcendental. (3) Material life includes birth and death; the spiritual realm is free from material rebirth.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.19](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/19/)

### 7. Describe the viewpoints of the impersonalist and the personalist in respect of their worship of the Lord. (SB 2.6.23)

**Answer:** The impersonalist concentrates on the Lord's impersonal effulgence or all-pervading aspect, whereas the personalist worships the Supreme Person through loving devotional service. The personalist understands His impersonal energy as dependent upon His eternal personal existence.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.23](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/23/)
