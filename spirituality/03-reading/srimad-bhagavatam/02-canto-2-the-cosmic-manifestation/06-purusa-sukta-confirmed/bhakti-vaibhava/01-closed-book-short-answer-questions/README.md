# Chapter 6: Puruṣa-sūkta Confirmed

## Closed-Book Short Answer Questions

**Bhakti Vaibhava · Unit 8 · Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam Canto 2**

**Source:** Bhakti Vaibhava Students’ Handbook, Unit 8; [Vedabase Chapter 6](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/).

> Study-oriented paraphrases, not verbatim quotations. Questions follow the handbook order. The personal-reflection question includes an explicitly marked model answer. Check exact Sanskrit terms against the linked verse and purport.

### 1. Which mantra is the beginning of all Vedic Mantras? (SB 2.6.1)

**Answer:** The Gāyatrī mantra is the beginning of all Vedic mantras.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.1](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/1/)

### 2. According to the purport of 2.6.1, how can our sensual activities be purified? (SB 2.6.1)

**Answer:** By engaging our senses in devotional service to Hṛṣīkeśa, the master of the senses, freed from material designations.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.1](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/1/)

### 3. What represents the back of the Lord in the universal form? (SB 2.6.10)

**Answer:** Frustration, ignorance, and immorality are associated with the Lord's back in the universal-form description.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.10](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/10/)

### 4. What is the duration of one day of Lord Brahmā, according to human calculation? (SB 2.6.11)

**Answer:** 4.32 billion human years (one thousand cycles of the four yugas).

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.11](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/11/)

### 5. Give the Sanskrit name of the philosophy propounded by Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. (SB 2.6.16)

**Answer:** Acintya-bhedābheda-tattva: inconceivable simultaneous oneness and difference.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.16](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/16/)

### 6. In the Caitanya-caritāmṛta, what are the total universes in the external potency of the Lord compared to? (SB 2.6.18)

**Answer:** A bag of mustard seeds: the innumerable universes are tiny compared with the Lord's unlimited potency.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.18](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/18/)

### 7. Name the three spheres into which the planetary systems are divided in the material world. (SB 2.6.19)

**Answer:** Svarga (higher), Martya (middle), and Pātāla (lower) planetary systems.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.19](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/19/)

### 8. What is the highest benefit that can be awarded to a human being? (SB 2.6.20)

**Answer:** Freedom from repeated birth and death through attainment of devotional service and the Lord's spiritual abode.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.20](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/20/)

### 9. Explain the analogy of the sun as used by Śrīla Prabhupāda in 2.6.22. (SB 2.6.22)

**Answer:** The sun remains in one place yet spreads sunshine everywhere; similarly the Lord remains transcendental while His energies pervade and sustain creation.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.22](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/22/)

### 10. Who is the first incarnation of the Supreme Lord? (SB 2.6.42)

**Answer:** The puruṣa incarnation (Mahā-Viṣṇu), the original manifestation for material creation.

**Reference:** [SB 2.6.42](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/6/42/)
