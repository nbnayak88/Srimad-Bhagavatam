## Chapter orientation

Explore divine appearances, spiritual identity, and the Bhāgavatam's transmission through attentive hearing.

Names below are editorial study themes, not authorized translations. Consult Vedabase for the verses, translations, and purports. Use the verse notes for personal reflection and Bhakti Vaibhava assessment preparation.

**Naming:** `verse-03.01-purusha` through `verse-03.44-hearing` — two digits after the decimal.
