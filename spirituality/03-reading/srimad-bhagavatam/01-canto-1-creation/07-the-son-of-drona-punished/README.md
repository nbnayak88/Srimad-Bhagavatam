**Total:** 58 numbered verses; 58 one-word-named study folders.

## Chapter orientation

Explore Vyāsa's devotional insight and a difficult judgment involving Aśvatthāmā, Arjuna, Draupadī, and Kṛṣṇa.

Names below are editorial study themes, not authorized translations. Consult Vedabase for the verses, translations, and purports. Use personal notes for reflection and Bhakti Vaibhava assessment preparation.

**Naming:** `verse-07.01-inquiry` through `verse-07.58-rites` — two digits after the decimal.

**Combined passages:** Vedabase groups texts 13–14 and 53–54. Separate numbered study folders link to their corresponding shared source pages.
