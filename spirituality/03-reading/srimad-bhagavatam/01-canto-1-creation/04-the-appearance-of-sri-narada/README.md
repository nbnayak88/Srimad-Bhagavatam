**Authorized source:** [Vedabase — SB 1.4](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/4/)

**Total:** 33 numbered verses; 33 one-word-named study folders.

## Chapter orientation

Explore the questions surrounding the Bhāgavatam's origins, Vyāsa's literary service and inner searching, and Nārada's visit.

Names below are editorial study themes, not authorized translations. Consult Vedabase for the verses, translations, and purports. Use personal notes for reflection and Bhakti Vaibhava assessment preparation.

**Naming:** `verse-04.01-appreciation` through `verse-04.33-reverence` — two digits after the decimal.

**Combined passages:** Vedabase groups texts 17–18 and 28–29. Separate numbered study folders link to their corresponding shared source pages.
