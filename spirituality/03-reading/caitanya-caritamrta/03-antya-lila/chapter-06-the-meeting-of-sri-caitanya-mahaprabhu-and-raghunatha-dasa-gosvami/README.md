# Antya-līlā Chapter 6 — The Meeting of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī

**Authorized chapter:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/antya/6/

**329 numbered texts** in individual study folders. The one-word themes are **provisional section-level editorial navigation labels**, not individually verified verse summaries or authorized translations. Translations and purports are linked, not reproduced.

## Chapter overview

Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī receives Nityānanda Prabhu's mercy through the Pānihāṭi chipped-rice festival, journeys to Jagannātha Purī, comes under Svarūpa Dāmodara's guidance, and receives a govardhana-śilā and guñjā-mālā from Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. The extraordinary austerities described are historical devotional accounts, not general health or dietary guidance.
