# Antya-līlā Chapter 8 — Rāmacandra Purī Criticizes the Lord

One-word themes are **provisional section-level editorial navigation labels**, not verified individual verse summaries or authorized translations. ## Chapter overview

The chapter contrasts the loving service rendered to Mādhavendra Purī by Īśvara Purī with Rāmacandra Purī's critical attitude. It recounts criticism of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu's eating, the devotees' concern, and the restoration of their normal devotional activities after Rāmacandra Purī departs.
