One-word themes are **provisional section-level editorial navigation labels**, not individually verified verse summaries or authorized translations. Translations and purports are linked rather than reproduced.

## Chapter overview

Vallabha Bhaṭṭa visits Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu in Jagannātha Purī, encounters His associates, and receives correction regarding scholarly pride and the authority of Śrīdhara Svāmī. The chapter also describes the Lord's affection for Gadādhara Paṇḍita and Vallabha Bhaṭṭa's renewed devotional association.
