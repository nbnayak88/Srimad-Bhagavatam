# Antya-līlā Chapter 13 — Pastimes with Jagadānanda Paṇḍita and Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī

**Authorized chapter:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/antya/13/

**139 numbered texts** in individual study folders. Vedabase combines **136–137** on one source page; each text has its own folder linking to the shared page. The one-word themes are **provisional section-level editorial navigation labels**, not individually verified verse summaries or authorized translations. Translations and purports are linked, not reproduced.

## Chapter overview

Jagadānanda Paṇḍita seeks to ease the Lord's discomfort with bedding, travels with permission to Vṛndāvana, visits Sanātana Gosvāmī, and returns with gifts. The chapter also recounts the Lord's response to hearing devotional singing and Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī's journey, service, instructions, and loving recitation of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam. Consult Vedabase for the authorized translations and purports.
