# Antya-līlā Chapter 19 — The Inconceivable Behavior of Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu

**Authorized chapter:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/antya/19/

**112 numbered texts** in individual study folders. The one-word themes are **provisional section-level editorial navigation labels**, not independently verified individual verse summaries or authorized translations. Translations and purports are linked, not reproduced.

## Chapter overview

Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu sends Jagadānanda Paṇḍita to console Śacīmātā and deliver prasādam, then receives Advaita Ācārya's enigmatic message. His separation from Kṛṣṇa intensifies; Svarūpa Dāmodara and other devotees care for Him, arranging for Śaṅkara Paṇḍita to remain near Him at night to prevent injury. In the Jagannātha-vallabha garden, the Lord experiences a vision of Kṛṣṇa and becomes absorbed in His fragrance. The descriptions of bodily injury are historical devotional narratives, not instructions for imitation. Consult Vedabase for authorized translations and purports.
