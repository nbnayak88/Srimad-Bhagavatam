**Authorized chapter:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/antya/11/

**108 numbered texts** in individual study folders. Vedabase combines **91–93** into a single source page; each text has its own folder linking to the shared page. One-word themes are **provisional section-level editorial navigation labels**, not independently verified verse summaries or authorized translations. Translations and purports are linked, not reproduced.

## Chapter overview

Haridāsa Ṭhākura chants the holy names and expresses his final devotional wish in Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu's presence. After his passing, the Lord personally carries and honors his body, participates in the burial by the sea, and organizes a prasādam festival in his memory. Consult Vedabase for the authorized text and purports.
