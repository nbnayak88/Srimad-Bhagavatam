## Chapter overview

During a night of devotional absorption, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu leaves His locked residence and is found unconscious among cows near Siṁha-dvāra, His limbs withdrawn like a tortoise. The devotees chant the holy names, and He regains external consciousness, describing a vision of Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana. The chapter further explores His longing to hear Kṛṣṇa's flute, voice and ornaments and His reflections on the gopīs' ecstatic love. Consult Vedabase for the authorized translations and purports.
