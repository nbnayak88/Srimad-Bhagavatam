# Antya-līlā Chapter 12 — The Loving Dealings Between Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and Jagadānanda Paṇḍita

The one-word themes are **provisional section-level editorial navigation labels**, not independently verified individual verse summaries or authorized translations. Translations and purports are linked, not reproduced.

## Chapter overview

Devotees travel from Bengal to Jagannātha Purī under Śivānanda Sena's care. The chapter recounts his exchanges with Lord Nityānanda, Śrīkānta Sena's response, Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu's affectionate dealings with the devotees, and Jagadānanda Paṇḍita's loving offering of sandalwood-scented oil. The Lord declines the oil in keeping with His renounced order; their affection is reconciled through Jagadānanda's cooking and the Lord's acceptance of prasādam.
