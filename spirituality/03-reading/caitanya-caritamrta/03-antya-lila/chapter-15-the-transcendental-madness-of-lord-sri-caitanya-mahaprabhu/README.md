# Antya-līlā Chapter 15 — The Transcendental Madness of Lord Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu

**Authorized chapter:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/antya/15/

**99 numbered texts** in individual study folders. The one-word themes are **provisional section-level editorial navigation labels**, not independently verified individual verse summaries or authorized translations. Translations and purports are linked, not reproduced.

## Chapter overview

Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu experiences intense transcendental longing for Kṛṣṇa after seeing Lord Jagannātha. A garden by the sea appears to Him as Vṛndāvana, and in the mood of the gopīs He inquires from trees, creepers and deer about Kṛṣṇa's whereabouts. Svarūpa Dāmodara's singing from the Gīta-govinda awakens ecstatic dancing and spiritual transformations. Consult Vedabase for the authorized translations and purports.
