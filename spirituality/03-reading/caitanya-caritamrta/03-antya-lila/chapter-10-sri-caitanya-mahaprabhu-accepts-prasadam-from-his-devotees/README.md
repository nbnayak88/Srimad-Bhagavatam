## Chapter overview

Devotees journey from Bengal to Jagannātha Purī. Rāghava Paṇḍita brings Damayantī's lovingly prepared food in the rāghavera jhāli. The chapter describes water pastimes at Narendra-sarovara, congregational chanting, Govinda's exemplary service, and the Lord accepting prasādam offered by devotees with affection.
