## Chapter overview

Kālidāsa's deep faith in the Vaiṣṇavas and his reverence for their remnants brings him the mercy of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. The chapter describes the youthful poetic gift of Purī dāsa (Kavi-karṇapūra), the Lord's longing for Kṛṣṇa during darśana of Jagannātha, and His relishing and distribution of Jagannātha's prasādam as nectar touched by Kṛṣṇa's lips. It concludes with His ecstatic reflections on Kṛṣṇa's flute and the gopīs' longing. Consult Vedabase for the authorized translations and purports.
