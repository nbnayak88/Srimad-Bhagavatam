**Authorized chapter:** https://vedabase.io/en/library/cc/antya/9/

## Chapter overview

Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka faces punishment over outstanding government revenue, is saved from execution and ultimately restored to office. Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu rejects misappropriation of public funds and instructs Gopīnātha and his family to discharge their financial obligations responsibly. The chapter distinguishes material prosperity from genuine devotional mercy.
