Vedabase combines **67–68, 90–91, 96–98, 142–143, and 144–146** on shared source pages; each numbered text has its own folder linked to the corresponding page. One-word themes are **provisional section-level editorial navigation labels**, not independently verified verse summaries or authorized translations. Translations and purports are linked, not reproduced.

## Chapter overview

Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu relishes the meanings of His eight Śikṣāṣṭaka prayers with Svarūpa Dāmodara and Rāmānanda Rāya. The prayers teach the glories of chanting the holy names, the Lord's merciful names, humility and tolerance, unmotivated devotional service, eternal servitude, ecstatic love, separation from Kṛṣṇa and unconditional devotion. Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja Gosvāmī then reflects on the unlimited pastimes, acknowledges his predecessors and benefactors, reviews the chapters of Antya-līlā, and concludes the Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta with prayers and a completion date. Consult Vedabase for the authorized translations and purports.
