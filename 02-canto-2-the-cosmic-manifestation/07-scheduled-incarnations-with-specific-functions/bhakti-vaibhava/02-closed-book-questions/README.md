# Chapter 7: Scheduled Incarnations with Specific Functions

## Closed-Book Questions

**Bhakti Vaibhava · Unit 8 · Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam Canto 2**

**Source:** Bhakti Vaibhava Students’ Handbook, Unit 8; [Vedabase Chapter 7](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/).

> Study-oriented paraphrases, not verbatim quotations. Questions follow the handbook order. The personal-reflection question includes an explicitly marked model answer. Check exact Sanskrit terms against the linked verse and purport.

### 1. Explain the example in 2.7.7 of how the Lord is āpta-kāma. (SB 2.7.7)

**Answer:** Āpta-kāma means completely self-satisfied, without unfulfilled desires. The Lord does not need anything from material creation or from living beings. His actions are voluntary pastimes and expressions of mercy, not attempts to fill a personal deficiency.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.7](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/7/)

### 2. Compare and contrast jaḍa-yoga with bhakti-yoga. (SB 2.7.10)

**Answer:** Jaḍa-yoga seeks control through material or mechanical processes of discipline and meditation. Bhakti-yoga directly engages the mind and senses in loving service to the Supreme Person. The former emphasizes techniques and control; the latter centers on devotion, surrender, and pleasing the Lord.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.10](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/10/)

### 3. Describe any two superhuman activities of the Lord and how they distinguish Him from ordinary beings. (SB 2.7.27)

**Answer:** Kṛṣṇa displayed the universal form to His mother Yaśodā and performed extraordinary childhood feats such as subduing powerful demons. These actions transcend the abilities of ordinary embodied beings and demonstrate His divine potency, not acquired material prowess.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.27](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/27/)

### 4. Why did the inhabitants of Vṛndāvana have no fear of the miseries of material existence? (SB 2.7.31)

**Answer:** Their consciousness was absorbed in loving remembrance and service of Kṛṣṇa. Protected by His personal care, they were not dominated by the anxieties that arise from material identification.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.31](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/31/)

### 5. Why did Lord Kṛṣṇa advise the inhabitants of Vrajabhūmi not to offer sacrifices to Indra? (SB 2.7.32)

**Answer:** He redirected their worship toward their immediate duties, cows, brāhmaṇas, and Govardhana Hill, teaching that the demigods are not independent of the Supreme Lord. Indra's pride was corrected when Kṛṣṇa protected Vraja.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.32](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/32/)

### 6. What benefits were obtained by those inimically fighting with the Lord or His expansions and associates at Kurukṣetra? (SB 2.7.34)

**Answer:** Those who died in direct contact with the Lord's presence and arrangement received extraordinary spiritual benefit and liberation, despite their inimical disposition. This does not make hostility a recommended path; loving devotion remains the proper practice.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.34](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/34/)

### 7. How was the son of Satyavatī compassionate to people of Kali-yuga? (SB 2.7.36)

**Answer:** Vyāsadeva divided and arranged Vedic knowledge and compiled accessible scriptures, including the Mahābhārata and Purāṇas, so people of diminished memory, qualification, and endurance could benefit spiritually.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.36](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/36/)

### 8. Explain the analogy of the ripened fruit in the purport to 2.7.51. (SB 2.7.51)

**Answer:** Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam is the ripened fruit of the Vedic desire tree: the mature, sweetest essence of Vedic revelation. It becomes especially relishable when transmitted through Śukadeva Gosvāmī and the genuine disciplic succession.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.51](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/51/)

### 9. Discuss the personal significance of 'Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam is the torchlight to see things in proper perspective'. (SB 2.7.52)

**Answer:** **Model personal reflection (adapt to your experience):** The Bhāgavatam helps me evaluate choices beyond temporary success and failure. Hearing its teachings reminds me that I am not merely the body, that my abilities are gifts, and that service to Kṛṣṇa gives life direction. Like a torch in darkness, it helps expose mistaken assumptions and guides my daily conduct.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.52](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/52/)

### 10. What is the difference between a pure devotee and a pseudo devotee with respect to hearing the Lord's activities? (SB 2.7.53)

**Answer:** A pure devotee hears the Lord's activities with faith, humility, and a desire for service, accepting authorized transmission. A pseudo devotee may seek entertainment, prestige, or material gain and neglect the purifying purpose of hearing.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.53](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/53/)
