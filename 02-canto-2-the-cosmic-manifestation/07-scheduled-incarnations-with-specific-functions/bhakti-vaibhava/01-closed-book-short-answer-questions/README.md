# Chapter 7: Scheduled Incarnations with Specific Functions

## Closed-Book Short Answer Questions

**Bhakti Vaibhava · Unit 8 · Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam Canto 2**

**Source:** Bhakti Vaibhava Students’ Handbook, Unit 8; [Vedabase Chapter 7](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/).

> Study-oriented paraphrases, not verbatim quotations. Questions follow the handbook order. The personal-reflection question includes an explicitly marked model answer. Check exact Sanskrit terms against the linked verse and purport.

### 1. What will be the effect of the 'modernized drilling of the earth' as explained in the purport? (SB 2.7.1)

**Answer:** Śrīla Prabhupāda warns that exploitative drilling for oil may disturb the earth's balance and cause harmful consequences. This is the purport's theological warning, not a demonstrated geophysical conclusion.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.1](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/1/)

### 2. What are the criteria for accepting an incarnation of the Lord as being authorized? (SB 2.7.2)

**Answer:** An incarnation must be described in revealed scripture and exhibit extraordinary divine activities corresponding to that description; popular claims alone do not establish an avatāra.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.2](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/2/)

### 3. What is the meaning of the word ātma-gatim? (SB 2.7.3)

**Answer:** The ultimate destination or path of self-realization, culminating in the Lord's shelter.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.3](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/3/)

### 4. List three characteristics of the Dhruva planet. (SB 2.7.8)

**Answer:** Dhruvaloka is an exalted, enduring abode; it serves as the fixed pivot for other stars; and Dhruva attained it by the Lord's mercy through devotion.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.8](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/8/)

### 5. According to varṇāśrama-dharma, what are the guiding principles for ruling the subjects? (SB 2.7.9)

**Answer:** A ruler protects citizens, upholds dharma, and governs for their material and spiritual welfare rather than personal gratification.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.9](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/9/)

### 6. How many Manus appear in one month of Brahmā? (SB 2.7.20)

**Answer:** 420 Manus: fourteen in each day of Brahmā, across thirty such days.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.20](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/20/)

### 7. What are the 'decorations of dead bodies' in the purport to 2.7.38? (SB 2.7.38)

**Answer:** Material accomplishments and external qualifications without genuine spiritual consciousness are likened to decorations on a dead body: outwardly impressive but lacking life's true purpose.

**Reference:** [SB 2.7.38](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/7/38/)
