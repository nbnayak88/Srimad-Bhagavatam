# Chapter 8: Questions by King Parīkṣit

## Closed-Book Questions

**Bhakti Vaibhava — Unit 8 | Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam Canto 2**

Source: Bhakti Vaibhava Students’ Handbook, Unit 8. [Vedabase Chapter 8](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/).

> Concise study paraphrases rather than verbatim purport quotations. Question order follows the handbook.

### 1. Explain how Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam is 'auspicious even for the non-devotees'. (2.8.2)

**Answer:** Hearing the Lord's transcendental topics benefits even those without established devotion. It gradually purifies consciousness and awakens interest in devotional service.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.2](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/2/)

### 2. Discuss the difference between hearing by professional men and the transcendental hearing of Mahārāja Parīkṣit. (2.8.3)

**Answer:** Professional recitation can be driven by money or performance, whereas Parīkṣit listened with sincerity, urgency and the desire for self-realization. His attentive inquiry to Śukadeva exemplifies transformative hearing.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.3](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/3/)

### 3. Explain the analogy of autumnal rain falling on muddy reservoirs. (2.8.5)

**Answer:** As muddy water becomes clear, hearing about the Lord removes impurities from the heart and clarifies spiritual understanding.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.5](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/5/)

### 4. Explain the analogy of the traveler satisfied at home after a troubled journey. (2.8.6)

**Answer:** A traveler becomes peaceful upon returning home; similarly, the soul finds genuine peace by taking shelter of the Lord after material wandering.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.6](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/6/)

### 5. Why is Brahmā, although called ajaḥ, completely dependent on the Lord's mercy? (2.8.9)

**Answer:** Brahmā's birth is extraordinary, but his existence, knowledge and power to create come from the Supreme Lord. Being 'unborn' in the ordinary sense does not make him independent.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.9](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/9/)

### 6. Explain the analogy of cleansing a small quantity of water by chemical processes. (2.8.5)

**Answer:** A small quantity may be treated by artificial means, while broader natural processes purify a reservoir. The purport compares limited purification efforts with the far-reaching cleansing power of hearing the Lord's message.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.5](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/5/)

### 7. Explain the two criteria for accepting an incarnation of the Lord as shown by Parīkṣit Mahārāja. (2.8.17)

**Answer:** An incarnation should be supported by authorized scriptural descriptions and recognized through the corresponding extraordinary divine characteristics and activities, not merely personal claims.

**Reference:** [SB 2.8.17](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/2/8/17/)
