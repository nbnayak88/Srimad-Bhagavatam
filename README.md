## How to use this repository

1. **Choose a Canto.** Open its folder README for an orientation and the direct Vedabase link.
2. **Read the source text.** Use Vedabase for the verses, translations, and purports.
3. **Study actively.** Record questions, key teachings, unfamiliar terms, and practical reflections in your own notes.
4. **Return to the text.** Revisit verses and purports as you reflect; distinguish the source text from your personal notes and interpretations.

## Suggested study-note template

For each reading session, consider recording:

- **Passage:** Canto, chapter, and verse(s)
- **Central theme:** What is the passage addressing?
- **Key terms:** Sanskrit terms and meanings to revisit
- **Purport insights:** Main points from the commentary
- **Questions:** What needs clarification or further reading?
- **Reflection and application:** How can the teaching inform conduct and devotional practice?
- **Cross-references:** Related passages elsewhere in Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam or other works
